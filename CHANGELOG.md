# BucketList — Changelog

## 1.3
- Added German (deDE) and Italian (itIT). All 11 official WoW locales are now covered.

## 1.2
- Automatic language detection based on the client's locale: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. Falls back to English if a translation is missing.

## 1.1 and earlier
Initial release.
- Personal in-game goals list: add, check off, reorder (right-click), and delete (with confirmation).
- Share your list to group, raid, guild, or chat via `/bucketlist` or `/bl`, or `/bl share <group|raid|guild|say|yell>`.
- Window position remembered between sessions.
